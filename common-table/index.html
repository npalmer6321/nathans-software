Our mission

Common Table builds a food system rooted in dignity and neighborliness. We believe fresh food is a right, not a privilege, and that the best way to fight hunger is to do it together.

  • Dignity first. Free, no-questions-asked access to food.
  • Local & fresh. Grown and rescued within ten miles.
  • Built together. Neighbors are volunteers, cooks and leaders.

What we do

Community Gardens

Six shared plots where families grow vegetables, swap seeds and learn from one another. No experience needed.

Food Rescue

Volunteer drivers collect surplus produce and bakery goods from local grocers and deliver it the same day.

Neighbor Suppers

A free community meal every Thursday. Come hungry, bring a friend, stay to help with dishes or don't.

Kitchen Skills

Free cooking and nutrition classes for teens and adults, with ingredients to take home.
