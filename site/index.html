Our mission

Small nonprofits, neighborhood groups and first-time learners are often priced out of the software they need. Nathan's Software exists to close that gap by building tools that are free, documented, and owned by the community that uses them.

  • Free forever. Everything we publish is open source and costs nothing to use.
  • Accessible by design. Our tools work on older devices, slow connections and with assistive technology.
  • Teach, don't just ship. We explain how things work so people can run and change their own software.

What we do

Open-Source Tools

Simple, well-documented apps for volunteer scheduling, donor tracking, event sign-ups and member directories, built for groups with no IT staff.

Free Coding Classes

Beginner-friendly workshops for teens and adults, with no prerequisites, no fees and all materials published online.

Tech Help for Nonprofits

Volunteer developers help small organizations launch websites, clean up their data and choose the right tools.

Mentorship

Pairing new programmers with experienced volunteers for code review, career advice and portfolio projects.
